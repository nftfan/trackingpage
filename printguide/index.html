Utilisez l'adresse LeuvenFlyers uniquement comme adresse de livraison des flyers, et non comme adresse de facturation.

Upload the print file

Click Upload een bestand and upload your print-ready PDF. Files up to 128 MB can be uploaded directly.

If your file is larger than 128 MB, the website states that it should be sent by WeTransfer to support@unitedwarehousegroup.com with your order number.

Eigen referentie is optional. Then click Bezorging | Stap 2.

5

Delivery and billing address

Under Kies bezorgen, select Bezorgen.

Request the LeuvenFlyers delivery address For privacy and security reasons, we do not publish the address of our storage location online.

Please email us to receive the current delivery address for your order.
info@leuvenflyers.com
Request delivery address by email

Once you receive the address from LeuvenFlyers, click Wijzig Afleveradres on the Print Media Group website and enter the address we provided.

Important: under Wijzig Factuuradres, enter your own company or personal billing details.

The LeuvenFlyers address should only be used as the flyer delivery address, not as your billing address.
