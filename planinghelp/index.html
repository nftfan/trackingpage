Campaign planner guide

Plan your flyer campaign with confidence.

Choose where you want to distribute, tell us where to collect your flyers, and share your plan with LeuvenFlyers. We’ll guide you through everything that follows.

Three simple steps

From your idea to a shared plan

The planner helps you tell us what you need. LeuvenFlyers will contact you about the practical details before a campaign goes ahead.

01

Choose an area

Search for your city or area. Tap the hexagons on the map to check the estimated buildings and book the zones you want in your plan. The flyer total updates as you build it.

02

Add your details

Enter the pickup address where you will hand over your printed flyers. Add extra flyers if needed, then enter your business name and email address. You can print a quote for reference.

03

Announce, then email us

Click Announce campaign so we can see your zones, quantities and pickup details. Keep the XX campaign ID shown in the planner. Email that ID to info@LeuvenFlyers.com, and we’ll help you with the next steps.

i

What happens after your email? LeuvenFlyers discusses the plan with you, explains the distribution process and coordinates the campaign when the details are agreed. Announcing your plan alone does not start distribution.

Inside the planner

What you can see and do

Map and flyer estimate

Select zones and see the estimated buildings and number of flyers required.

Campaign summary

View total flyers, delivered flyers and the price shown in the planner.

Pickup and shifts

Set your pickup address and see the shifts created from the flyer quantity.

Progress and photos

Once work begins, pickup and delivery markers and available proof photos appear on the map.

Your part

Plan, print and hand over

Tell us where you want to distribute and where to collect the flyers. Once the campaign is agreed:

  • Arrange for your flyers to be printed.
  • Have them ready at your chosen pickup address.
  • Give them to the distributor when they arrive.
Our part

We coordinate the distribution

LeuvenFlyers has a network of trained flyer distributors who can accept the available shifts in your campaign. We handle the work behind the scenes:

  • Coordinate shifts and flyer pickup.
  • Communicate with you throughout the process.
  • Manage and check distribution to maintain campaign quality.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers

Does “Announce campaign” place an order?

No. It shares your planned requirements with LeuvenFlyers. We will discuss the details with you before a final campaign is arranged.

What should I do after announcing?

Email info@LeuvenFlyers.com and include your XX campaign ID. We’ll use it to find your plan and explain what happens next.

Who prints the flyers?

You arrange printing and have the flyers ready. LeuvenFlyers manages the distribution once the campaign details are agreed.

How does pickup work?

Enter your preferred pickup address in the planner. After the campaign is arranged, a distributor will come to that address to collect the printed flyers. We will communicate with you about the arrangements.

How do I know how many flyers to print?

The planner estimates a flyer total from the zones you select and any extra flyers you add. Review the quantity with us before printing.

Can I get a quote?

Yes. Add your business name and email address, then use Print quote / Save PDF in the planner. The quote shows the planned details and price; it is not an invoice or final booking.

How can I follow the distribution?

Open your saved campaign using its XX ID. The planner shows delivered flyers, pickup and distribution progress, map markers and available proof photos as work is recorded.

Can I ask for help before planning?

Of course. Email info@LeuvenFlyers.com. You can also use the Help button in the planner to return to this guide.

Ready to share your plan?

Announce it in the planner, then email us your XX campaign ID. We’ll take it from there together.
