FLEXIBLE CAMPAIGN SERVICES

Four ways to work with us

Choose the setup that works best for your business. We take care of the delivery.

OPTION01

We collect your printed flyers

Already have printed flyers at your address? Our promoters collect them directly from you and distribute them in your selected areas.

HOW IT WORKS
  1. Your printed flyers
  2. We collect
  3. We distribute
OPTION02

Send your printed flyers to us

Ship or deliver your printed flyers to our address. We receive them, coordinate the promoters and manage the full distribution.

HOW IT WORKS
  1. You send
  2. We receive
  3. We distribute
OPTION03

Send a PDF. We print and distribute.

Have a ready-to-print flyer design? Send us the PDF. We arrange the printing and distribute the flyers in your chosen areas.

HOW IT WORKS
  1. Your PDF
  2. We print
  3. We distribute
OPTION04

Complete campaign management

Simply tell us how many flyers you need and where you want to reach people. We handle the design, printing and distribution from start to finish.

HOW IT WORKS
  1. Your brief
  2. We design & print
  3. We distribute
