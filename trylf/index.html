Frequently asked questions

Is the trial free?

No. The 1,000-flyer trial is a paid service, but payment is only required after successful delivery. Ask us for a quote by email.

How much does the trial cost?

We provide the price by email. Send us your planned delivery area and request a quote for 1,000 A5 flyers including printing and distribution.

What do I need to send?

Send your flyer artwork as an A5 PDF by email and let us know that you would like the trial service. Include your selected planning area when possible.

Can I choose where my flyers are delivered?

Yes. Select your preferred hexagons in our planning tool. We will confirm the distribution area for your 1,000-flyer trial.
